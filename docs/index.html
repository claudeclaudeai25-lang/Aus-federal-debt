Notes on the data

  • Cash view (default): receipts and payments of the Australian Government general government sector, the basis of the headline underlying cash balance. The table's balance column is receipts less payments, which equals the underlying cash balance except between 2005–06 and 2019–20, when net Future Fund earnings are also deducted.
  • Accrual view: revenue and expenses, recorded when earned or incurred rather than when cash changes hands. The difference is the net operating balance. Accrual data begin in 1996–97.
  • Gross debt is the face value of Australian Government Securities (AGS) on issue at 30 June, including a small amount held on behalf of the states and the Northern Territory. Net debt, which deducts financial assets, is lower and is not shown.
  • Treasury revises the historical series in each edition to improve comparability over time (for example for accounting classification changes), so figures can differ slightly from those first published for a year.
  • All values are nominal, not adjusted for inflation or the size of the economy.
